TL;DR ReState is a unified iterative reasoning framework that reformulates multimodal reasoning from a stage-driven pipeline into a state-driven dynamic process.

01

Stage-centric vs. state-centric

Most existing learning-based MCoT methods follow a stage-centric paradigm, assigning reasoning behaviors to predefined stages with separate optimization. This fragments reasoning and introduces a gap between the reasoning states used during training and those encountered at inference. ReState reformulates multimodal reasoning as a state-driven dynamic process, unifying reasoning construction, preservation, and correction through a shared state-update operator.

02

Method: URSM + CRSL

URSM unifies reasoning construction, correct reasoning preservation, and erroneous reasoning correction as state transitions learned jointly by a single MLLM. CRSL incorporates self-generated historical reasoning states during training and recursively updates the current state at inference, mitigating the training–inference reasoning-state gap and enabling iterative self-refinement without additional critics, reflection modules, or separate correction stages.

Unified Reasoning State Modeling

URSM: one operator, three behaviors

(St+1, Ât+1) = Mθ(X, St),   St ∈ {φ, R+, R−}

X = {image, question, options, caption}; φ is represented by the token [EMPTY_SOLUTION]. In each training epoch, URSM constructs two views per sample: one with φ and one with a non-empty reasoning state, sampled from either R+ or R− with equal probability.

    VersaCoT-10K

    VersaCoT-10K contains 10,000 single-image multiple-choice samples spanning eight heterogeneous visual reasoning domains. Its construction consists of three stages: data collection and standardization, CoT annotation and quality control, and reasoning-state augmentation. The resulting dataset provides diverse supervision for multi-domain reasoning and reasoning-state refinement. Each domain contains 1,250 samples, with a 7 : 1 : 2 train / validation / test split.

    10,000samples
    8domains × 1,250
    170.9avg CoT words (median 162, range 50–537)

    1. 1Collect & standardize

      Reasoning-intensive samples from 8 public benchmarks, unified into one multiple-choice format with options, answer, and image caption.

    2. 2Annotate & filter

      Gemini-3-Flash writes visually grounded CoTs. Rule filters (200–4,000 chars, answer-leak phrases) plus a separate Gemini-3.5-Flash judge (all three binary checks must pass, and each of the five scores must be ≥ 8).

    3. 3Augment states

      Following RAD, each CoT gets positive states (diverse, semantically consistent) and hard negative states (similar, semantically reversed). Historical states Rh are generated online during training.

    Results

    Under matched backbone and training settings across three backbones, ReState outperforms Direct SFT, Multimodal-CoT, and MIND by 0.90%–4.60% on VersaCoT-10K, 0.83%–2.87% on ScienceQA, and 0.18%–13.07% on M³CoT.

    Consistent improvements

    Best average accuracy in all 9 matched-backbone settings across 3 datasets and 3 backbones. For example, on VersaCoT-10K with Qwen3.5-9B, average accuracy improves from 77.80% with Direct SFT to 81.90% with ReState.

    Component ablation

    URSM improves accuracy by 0.50%–2.55% on VersaCoT-10K. Adding either training-time self-history states or inference-time iterative self-refinement brings further gains, with the full framework performing best across all three backbones.

    Iterative refinement

    One refinement round improves accuracy by 1.10%–2.65% on VersaCoT-10K. Most gains occur in the first few rounds, followed by stabilization with minor fluctuations.

    Main results

    Component Ablation and Iterative Refinement

    On VersaCoT-10K, URSM improves accuracy by 0.50%–2.55% over Direct SFT. Adding SHS or ISR separately to URSM adds 0.30%–2.15% or 0.45%–0.55%, respectively. The full framework performs best across all three backbones, reaching 77.05%, 79.25%, and 81.90% on Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Qwen3-VL-8B, and Qwen3.5-9B, respectively.

    Across VersaCoT-10K, ScienceQA, and M³CoT, iterative refinement delivers most of its gains in the first few rounds, after which accuracy stabilizes with minor fluctuations. The figures below summarize the component ablations and refinement trajectories.

    Same question, four methods

    Qualitative comparisons on VersaCoT-10K, ScienceQA, and M³CoT illustrate how visual evidence shapes reasoning outcomes. In these examples, Direct SFT, Multimodal-CoT, and MIND produce fluent reasoning but reach incorrect answers after misreading visual relationships, relying on misleading local cues, or introducing unsupported details. ReState identifies relevant evidence and integrates it into correct reasoning, from tracing diagram relationships and comparing concentrations to recognizing species and shapes.
